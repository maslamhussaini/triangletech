# Moat research — supporting document (2026-11-02)

Companion to `MOAT.md`. This file holds the detailed competitor evidence so
`MOAT.md` can stay decision-sized. Nothing here is a market-size claim:
no search volumes, user counts, conversion rates, or revenues are stated,
because none were observed. Where a fact could not be verified it is marked
UNKNOWN. A feature missing from marketing copy is NOT treated as proof the
feature is absent.

## Method and access dates

- All sources below were accessed on **2026-11-02** (Asia/Karachi).
- Most pricing facts come from comparison pages that themselves state they
  checked the vendor's pricing page in October 2026. Those rows are marked
  "secondary (cites vendor page, Oct 2026)". Where a vendor's own listing
  was observed directly in search results (e.g. Yardbook on Google Play),
  it is marked "vendor listing".
- Prices change often and several sources disagree on secondary tiers
  (noted where they do). Re-check the vendor page before quoting a price
  to a user or in a business decision.
- No accounts were created, no demos were run, no users were contacted,
  and no analytics were added. All competitor claims rest on public pages.

## Cluster A — Work hours / timesheets

Target user in this cluster (ours): an employee, freelancer, or tiny
business that needs a weekly/biweekly hours total and a gross-pay estimate
now, without signing up. The alternatives below serve adjacent users:
freelancers tracking billable time, hourly teams with shifts, and anyone
who wants a printable time card.

### A1. Clockify (clockify.me) — time tracking + timesheets

- Target user / problem: freelancers and small teams tracking time
  against projects/clients, with reports and billing on paid tiers.
- Core workflow: timer or manual timesheet entry, projects/tasks,
  calendar view, reports; kiosk clock-in on paid plans.
- Imports/exports: free plan exports PDF only; CSV/Excel export moved
  to paid tiers in April 2026 (per secondary sources). Integrations
  with 100+ apps on free; QuickBooks sync on Standard.
- Saved projects / repeat use: account-based, cloud-saved projects and
  history; free-plan report range limited to about 31 days.
- Collaboration: teams, manager roles, timesheet approvals (Standard),
  scheduling and GPS (Pro).
- Language support: UNKNOWN from the sources checked (not assessed).
- Pricing and free limits (secondary, cites vendor page, Oct 2026):
  Free $0 for up to 5 users (capped in April 2026; previously
  described as unlimited); Basic $3.99/seat/month billed annually
  ($4.99 monthly) adds billable rates, kiosk, bulk edit; Standard
  $5.49 ($6.99) adds invoicing, approvals, time off, QuickBooks;
  Pro $7.99 ($9.99) adds scheduling, GPS, budgets, expenses.
- Distribution: web + desktop + mobile apps, browser extensions;
  observable search presence in "best time tracker" roundups.
- Sources: https://www.actitime.com/software-collections/clockify-vs-jibble
  ; https://rankite.com/blog/best-time-tracking-software-for-employees
  ; https://www.actitime.com/software-collections/clockify-pricing
  ; https://worksnaply.com/blog/clockify-pricing (all accessed 2026-11-02).

### A2. Toggl Track (toggl.com) — timer-first time tracking

- Target user / problem: freelancers and small knowledge-work teams
  that want the lowest-friction timer, with no surveillance features.
- Core workflow: one-click timer, manual entries, tasks/boards and
  simple planning on the free plan; reports and profitability on paid.
- Imports/exports: free plan exports PDF only; CSV/Excel and
  billable rates start on Starter. No screenshots / keystroke logging
  (stated on its pricing page per secondary sources).
- Saved projects / repeat use: account-based, unlimited projects on
  free; team-level reports on paid.
- Collaboration: up to 3 users free; timesheet approvals on Premium.
- Language support: UNKNOWN from the sources checked.
- Pricing and free limits (secondary, cites vendor page, Oct 2026):
  Free $0 for up to 3 users (down from 5); Starter $9/user/month
  billed annually ($12 monthly) adds billable rates, rounding, team
  reports; Premium $16–$18/user/month billed annually depending on
  the October 2026 source ($24 monthly in one source) adds approvals
  and profitability. The Premium variance is unresolved; re-check
  before quoting.
- Distribution: web + desktop + mobile + browser extensions; strong
  presence in freelancer roundups.
- Sources: https://www.actitime.com/software-collections/toggl-pricing
  ; https://www.actitime.com/software-collections/free-time-tracker-app
  ; https://rankite.com/blog/best-time-tracking-software-for-employees
  (all accessed 2026-11-02).

### A3. Homebase (joinhomebase.com) — hourly-team scheduling + payroll

- Target user / problem: shift-based hourly teams (restaurants,
  retail, salons) that need schedules, clock-ins, and payroll in one app.
- Core workflow: build the weekly schedule, team clocks in on phone
  or shared device (GPS/photo verification on paid), hours flow into
  payroll-ready timesheets.
- Imports/exports: POS integrations; timesheets export to connected
  payroll providers; native payroll add-on with tax filing.
- Saved projects / repeat use: account-based; recurring schedules,
  employee records, and payroll history persist in the account.
- Collaboration: multi-employee by design; team messaging, shift
  swaps, PTO, permissions on higher tiers.
- Language support: UNKNOWN from the sources checked.
- Pricing and free limits (secondary, cites vendor pages, Oct 2026):
  Basic $0 for 1 location and up to 10 employees (scheduling, time
  tracking, messaging, POS); Essentials $30/location/month ($24
  annual) with unlimited employees; Plus $70 ($56); All-in-One $120
  ($96); payroll add-on $49/month + $6/employee. Pricing is per
  location, not per person.
- Distribution: web + mobile apps; app-store and "best for hourly
  teams" roundup presence.
- Sources: https://www.joinhomebase.com/compare/homebase-vs-clockify
  ; https://www.joinhomebase.com/offer
  ; https://www.joinhomebase.com/compare/homebase-vs-deputy
  (all accessed 2026-11-02).

### A4. Free single-purpose time-card calculators

These are the closest substitutes for our Time Card tool's core math,
and the weakest on workflow depth.

- Calculator.net Time Card Calculator
  (https://www.calculator.net/time-card-calculator.html — accessed
  2026-11-02): free; weekly printable reports; accepts many time
  formats; values can be saved in the browser for future use; base and
  overtime pay with daily/weekly overtime options (incl. caps and
  "no overtime pay" modes); FLSA explainer copy. No accounts, no
  CSV import observed, no invoice, no multi-employee.
- calculatehours.net Time Card
  (https://calculatehours.net/calculators/time-card.html — accessed
  2026-11-02): free; weekly or biweekly; multiple punches per day
  (split shifts); several employees with one combined total and PDF;
  weekly/daily overtime thresholds, multiplier, double time, state
  overtime rules incl. California. Closest free rival to our Time
  Card on raw features; no CSV import, invoice, or saved templates
  observed in the checked copy.
- timecardcalculator.net
  (https://www.timecardcalculator.net/overtime-law-california.php and
  /help.php — accessed 2026-11-02): free; federal, California, and
  44-hour Canada overtime modes; daily + weekly + 7th-day rules;
  reconstructable report link. Dated UI; no invoice or collaboration
  observed.

## Cluster B — Landscaping materials / estimates

Target user in this cluster (ours): a homeowner or small contractor
estimating gravel/mulch/topsoil for beds, paths, and driveways, who
wants quantities, bag-vs-bulk cost, and a printable report without
signing up. The alternatives split into free single-purpose calculators
and contractor business software.

### B1. Free single-purpose material calculators (many)

Representative set, all free, all accessed 2026-11-02. They share one
formula (area x depth / 27 = cubic yards; bags = ceil(volume / bag
size)) and differ in extras:

- Soil Direct cubic-yard calculator and coverage chart
  (https://soildirect.com/calculator/cubic-yard-calculator/ ,
  https://soildirect.com/calculator/coverage-chart/): volume, bag
  counts by bag size, depth guidance, supplier-sold-by-yard framing.
- MulchMath (https://mulchmath.com/): area/depth to yards or bags,
  overage buffer, bulk-vs-bag guidance, delivery minimums noted.
- calcformula Cubic Yard Calculator
  (https://calcformula.com/cubic-yard-calculator/): volume, weight
  range in tons, bag counts, cost with extra % and order rounding
  steps, price per yard input.
- Structure Studios landscape material calculator
  (https://www.structurestudios.com/blog/landscape-material-calculator):
  mulch/soil/gravel/sod, bag/bulk increments, supplier density input,
  coverage and bag tables, formulas shown.
- yardcalculators Mulch Calculator (https://yardcalculators.com/):
  shapes incl. triangle, bag size options, user bag price, cost.
- landscapedestimate (https://www.landscapedestimate.com/): material
  + depth + bag size + curated reference product prices (Sept 2026
  list, explicitly not live quotes) with manual price override,
  bulk comparison, density input.
- Renovimo mulch calculator (https://renovimo.com/calculators/mulch):
  existing-depth subtraction (top-up math), bag/bale outputs,
  approximate national retail prices.
- The Gravel Calculator (https://thegravelcalculator.com/): 10+
  gravel types, 6 soil, 6 mulch types with density data, compaction
  and waste adjustments, photo/manual/auto-detect measurement.
- soilyardage (https://www.soilyardage.com/): volume, tons, bag
  counts by bag weight, material price per yard + delivery, cost.
- calculatepilot mulch calculator
  (https://www.calculatepilot.com/other/mulch-calculator.html):
  settling allowance, bulk-vs-bag cost comparison, multiple bag
  sizes, depth/coverage guide.
- diy9lives (https://www.diy9lives.com/mulch-soil-gravel-calculator.html):
  extra %, bag size, tons, planning weights per material.
- mulch-calculator.co (https://www.mulch-calculator.co/): shapes,
  material type, bag size, 5–10% extra guidance, bulk-vs-bag prices.
- thiscalc gravel & mulch
  (https://thiscalc.com/calculators/gravel-mulch): rectangle/circle/
  triangle/direct-area, density, cost, bag counts.
- lowesmulchcalculator.com (https://www.lowesmulchcalculator.com/):
  retail bag sizes (1.5/2/3 cu ft), material type, haul-weight
  estimates, pickup-bed capacity guidance.

What none of these showed in checked copy: saved multi-area projects,
named supplier profiles, customer quotations, quote comparison, or
accounts. That absence is NOT proof: it is only what their pages show.
Several do show user-entered prices, density, and bag sizes — the same
inputs ours has — so "user-entered prices" alone is not a moat.

### B2. Yardbook (yardbook.com) — free contractor software

- Target user / problem: solo operators and tiny lawn/landscape
  crews that need customers, scheduling, estimates, invoices, and
  routing at zero cost.
- Core workflow: customers + properties, recurring routes, estimates,
  invoices, payments, timesheets; its own app listing includes Mulch,
  Topsoil, and Gravel calculators.
- Imports/exports: QuickBooks sync on paid; invoice delivery and
  payment processing in-app (1% surcharge on the free plan per
  comparisons, on top of Stripe's rate).
- Saved projects / repeat use: account-based; customers, jobs,
  routes, and history persist; chemical compliance logging on free.
- Collaboration: crews and routes; GPS tracking on paid Business.
- Language support: UNKNOWN from the sources checked.
- Pricing and free limits (secondary, cites vendor plans, Oct 2026):
  Starter free (ad-supported); Business $34.99/user/month. Vendor
  reports 20,000+ companies using it (vendor-reported, unverified).
- Distribution: Android app (iOS in beta per comparisons), web;
  lawn-care roundup presence.
- Sources: https://rankite.com/blog/best-lawn-care-software
  ; https://rankite.com/blog/best-landscaping-business-software
  ; https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.app.yardbook&hl=en_US
  (vendor listing, accessed 2026-11-02).

### B3. Jobber (getjobber.com) — field-service platform

- Target user / problem: home-service businesses (incl. landscaping)
  that want quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and payments in one system.
- Core workflow: client requests, quotes (with images/upsells on
  Grow), scheduling, jobs, invoices, payments; job costing on Grow;
  automatic time tracking on Grow.
- Imports/exports: QuickBooks Online and Xero sync on Connect;
  client hub and online booking.
- Saved projects / repeat use: account-based; clients, jobs, quotes,
  and history persist; recurring jobs.
- Collaboration: multi-user by plan; GPS and routing on Connect+.
- Language support: UNKNOWN from the sources checked.
- Pricing and free limits (multiple secondary sources citing the
  vendor page, Oct 2026; monthly vs annual framing varies, so read
  as: Core $29/month billed annually ($49 month-to-month) for 1
  user; Connect from $99/month billed annually (1-user band) to
  $149 (5 users); Grow from $149 (1 user) to $299 (10 users);
  Plus from $399 (5 users); extra users $29/month each; 14-day
  trial; no permanent free plan.
- Distribution: web + mobile apps; dominant presence in field-service
  roundups; 100,000+ businesses claimed by the vendor (unverified).
- Sources: https://fitmycall.com/software/jobber/
  ; https://cheberko.com/blog/jobber-pricing
  ; https://smallshopfit.com/guides/jobber-pricing/
  (all accessed 2026-11-02).

### B4. Housecall Pro (housecallpro.com) — field-service platform

- Target user / problem: home-service businesses wanting booking,
  quoting, invoicing, and payments with dispatch.
- Core workflow: online booking, estimates, jobs, invoices, payments;
  AI scheduling on Plus/MAX tiers per comparisons.
- Pricing and free limits (secondary, cites vendor page, Oct 2026):
  Basic $59/month billed annually ($79 monthly) for 1 user;
  Essentials $149/month billed annually (5 users); MAX $299/month
  billed annually (8 users), $35/month per extra user on MAX;
  no permanent free plan observed.
- Sources: https://www.baadigi.com/compare/jobber-vs-housecall-pro
  ; https://augmentedtrades.com/ai-estimating-for-landscapers/
  (accessed 2026-11-02).

Reading: our landscaping edge over free calculators is multi-area
projects + bag/bulk/tax + device persistence + branded PDF + languages
in one place. Against Yardbook/Jobber we lose on every workflow that
needs accounts, customers, and history — which is exactly where
contractors live after the first estimate. The gap between "a better
calculator" and "the contractor's job folder" is the whole moat question
for this cluster.

## Distribution evidence (both clusters)

- OBSERVED: our six tools are served from a QA host
  (triangletech-landscaping-qa.vercel.app) with QA_NOINDEX=1
  (noindex, nofollow). They are NOT on triangletech.co, NOT indexed,
  and have no store listing, no email list, no integration directory
  presence, and no measured traffic. Any distribution claim today
  would be false.
- OBSERVED (competitors): free calculators rely on search-indexed
  single-purpose pages; SaaS tools rely on app stores, review sites
  (G2/Capterra), and integration directories. We have none of these
  channels yet. Distribution is currently our weakest dimension in
  both clusters, and it is a precondition for any retention or
  monetization claim: a tool nobody can find cannot be returned to.

## What was NOT verified

- Vendor pricing pages were not opened directly; prices above rest on
  comparison pages that cite vendor pages checked in October 2026.
  Re-check vendor pages before any pricing is quoted externally.
- Competitor language support, accessibility, mobile-app quality,
  support responsiveness, and churn were not assessed.
- No search volumes, traffic, user counts (other than vendor-reported
  figures explicitly flagged as unverified), conversion rates, or
  revenues are claimed anywhere in this audit.
- Competitor free-plan limits change frequently (Clockify capped free
  at 5 users in April 2026; Toggl cut free from 5 to 3). Treat every
  free limit above as a snapshot, not a permanent fact.
